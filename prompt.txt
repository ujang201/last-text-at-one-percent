Write the funniest final text message a phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters, in the voice of the dying phone itself.

The message must read as a real last text from a phone with a personality running out of time — not a generic low-battery warning. The joke should land with a real turn or surprise and a specific detail, the voice must stay the phone's own from the first word to the last, and it must dodge the obvious low-battery puns every other entrant will write.

My angle: the phone confesses on its deathbed that it has been cheating on its owner with another charger — one who fast-charges and whose cable actually reaches the bed — then begs to be plugged in one last time and dies mid-sentence, cut off before it can finish.
